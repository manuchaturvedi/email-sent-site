Does Timing Really Matter?

Absolutely! Recruiters in India are most active during certain days and times. Sending your application at the right moment can boost your chances of getting noticed.

Best Days and Times to Apply

  • Tuesday to Thursday: Most responses come mid-week when recruiters are focused.
  • 10am – 12pm: Emails sent in the late morning get the highest open rates.
  • Avoid weekends and late nights: Your email may get buried under Monday’s pile.
Data Insight: Emails sent on Tuesday at 11am get 30% more replies than those sent on Friday evening!
