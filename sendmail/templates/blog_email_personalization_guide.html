Why Personalization Matters in India

Recruiters in India receive hundreds of generic job applications. Personalizing your email shows you’ve done your homework and truly care about the company.

How to Personalize Your Job Application Emails

  1. Use the Recruiter’s Name: “Hi Ms. Singh” is more effective than “Dear Sir/Madam”.
  2. Mention the Company: Reference recent news, values, or projects.
  3. Highlight Relevant Skills: Relate your experience to the job description.
  4. Keep It Brief: Busy managers appreciate concise, focused emails.
Sample Template:
Subject: Application for [Role] at [Company]
Hi [Recruiter Name],
I’m excited to apply for [Role] at [Company] because…

Regards,
[Your Name]
