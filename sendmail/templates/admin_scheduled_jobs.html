About Scheduled Jobs

Scheduled jobs automatically scrape LinkedIn for job posts based on your criteria at specified intervals.

Cron Format: minute hour day month weekday (e.g., 0 9 * * * = daily at 9:00 AM)

Examples: 0 */6 * * * (every 6 hours) | 0 8,20 * * * (8 AM & 8 PM) | 0 9 * * 1 (Mondays at 9 AM)
