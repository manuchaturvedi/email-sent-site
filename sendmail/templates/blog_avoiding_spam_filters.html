Why Do Job Application Emails Go to Spam?

Spam filters are designed to protect recruiters from unwanted emails. Unfortunately, even genuine job applications can get caught if you’re not careful.

Common Mistakes by Indian Job Seekers

  • Using words like “FREE”, “Urgent”, or too many exclamation marks.
  • Sending from unprofessional email addresses (e.g., cooldude123@...)
  • Attaching large files instead of sharing links to your portfolio.
  • Not testing your email before sending.
Checklist to Avoid Spam:
  • Use a clear, professional subject line.
  • Keep your message concise and relevant.
  • Send via a reputable sender (your domain or JustMailIt delivery).
  • Limit attachments and use links when possible.
  • Test your email by sending it to yourself first.
